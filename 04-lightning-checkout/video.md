## Full lesson outline (about 15 minutes, to record)
1. **Hook, 0:00–1:00.** A shop site that ends with "send proof of transfer on WhatsApp". What if the site could confirm the payment itself?
2. **Four ideas, 1:00–4:00.** Invoices, server-side prices, server-side "paid", and the preimage as the receipt.
3. **Run the demo, 4:00–7:00.** `npm run demo`, the test wallets it makes, a paid order, the receipt.
4. **Read the code, 7:00–12:00.** `priceCart`, `RateService`, `Checkout.status`, `checkPermissions`.
5. **Go live, 12:00–14:00.** A receive-only NWC connection from the vendor's wallet, HTTPS, a ₦200 test item.
6. **Wrap, 14:00–15:00.** Exercises and module 5.
